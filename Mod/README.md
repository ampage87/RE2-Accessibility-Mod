# Resident Evil 2 Accessibility Mod (RE2Access) 0.9.23

Speech through Windows SAPI for Resident Evil 2 (Sourcenext 1.10 with Classic REbirth).
The mod is `RE2Access.asi`, loaded by the ASI loader (`dsound.dll`) that the setup script installs.

## Install and test

1. Close the game.
2. Run `Deploy.vbs` in the mod folder. It builds the mod with Visual Studio if it is installed (otherwise it uses the prebuilt copy), then copies it into the game folder listed in `Game Install Info.txt`. If Windows asks for permission (User Account Control), answer Yes.
3. A message box tells you whether it worked. The deploy log is `Logs\deploy_latest.log`.
4. Start the game. You should hear "Resident Evil 2 accessibility mod 0.9.23 loaded."
5. The game log is written to both the game folder and the mod folder's `Logs\RE2Access.log`.

## Keys

- `-` and `=`: previous and next thing in the catalog (name, then "N of M")
- `[` and `]`: change catalog category (All, Exits, Items, Objects, Enemies). Every room starts on All. The catalog keeps its order while you are in a room: things are numbered nearest first once the room has finished setting up, and anything that appears later is added at the end and announced ("New: Federal police report").
- Backspace: directions to the selected thing
- `\`: walk there automatically (running on long stretches, steering around awake enemies; it stops only when an enemy is right next to you or there is no way around one). It squeezes through narrow gaps between furniture and takes stairs and slopes to things on another level. Shift+`\`: turn to face it
- T: turn to the nearest enemy. Shift+T: next enemy
- `'` (apostrophe): where am I (room name)
- H: health
- Y: time left on an escape countdown ("3 minutes 12 seconds left"). When a countdown starts the mod says so, then says the time left at each whole minute and at 30 and 10 seconds.
- I: inventory
- E: enemies nearby
- Shift+E: get away from the nearest enemy: walks you, running, to the open place in the room furthest from it by a way that keeps clear of it (not into a corner), then says where it is ("Away. Super Tyrant 9 metres, behind you."). Then T faces it again to shoot. Made for boss fights.
- `;`: walls around you
- `,`: compass direction
- `.`: explore the room (walk to the nearest place not yet visited)
- M: the map from where you are: the area, how many of its rooms you have been in, this room's doors (where each leads, locked or not and which key opens it, visited or not), the items still lying here, a typewriter or item box
- Shift+M: open or close the map of every room in the game. While it is open:
  - `-` and `=`: rooms in the area ("visited" or "not visited yet")
  - `[` and `]`: areas (the city, each police station floor, the roof, the basement, the underground, the sewers and onwards)
  - `'`: the selected room in full: its doors and their locks, items still there, typewriter, item box
  - Backspace: the way there from where you are, room by room, with any locked doors on the way (doors whose key you carry count as open)
  - `\`: travel there. The mod walks you to each door on the way and goes through it, room after room, saying how many doors are left, until "You have reached ...". A door that asks first ("Will you go down the ladder?") is answered Yes for you. An enemy close by, a locked door, a danger area or any other question from the game pauses the journey ("Travel paused"); the next door stays selected and `\` carries on. Moving yourself also pauses it.
  - `/`: map key help
  The map shows more than the game's own map does: every room, even before you find the area's map, and the items left in each room.
- `` ` `` (grave accent): repeat the last thing spoken
- `/`: key help
- F1: next voice (SAPI)
- Shift F1: speech output. Cycles Automatic (your screen reader if one is running, otherwise SAPI), each screen reader that is running (JAWS, NVDA and others), and SAPI. The choice is saved as speech_output in RE2Access.ini. Ducking, rate, volume and voice (F1 to F4) apply to SAPI only; with a screen reader, use its own settings.
- F3 and F4: speech rate down and up
- Shift+F3 and Shift+F4: volume down and up, from 0 to 200 percent. Above 100 the mod makes the speech louder itself (a limiter keeps it from distorting). The movie and introduction descriptions follow this volume too. During the narrated introduction before a new game, these keys set the introduction descriptions' own volume instead (they start at 50 percent of the speech volume, so the narrator can be heard).
- F2: ducking on or off. While the mod is talking, the game's own sound (music, effects, movie sound) drops, then comes back up about a third of a second after the mod stops. On by default. Never during the narrated introduction.
- Shift+F2: how low the game's sound goes while the mod talks: 20, 35, 50, 65 (the default) or 80 percent.
- F5: unlimited health on or off
- F6: unlimited ammo on or off (for the equipped weapon)
- F10: version
- F11: write a diagnostic snapshot to the log

Mod keys interrupt speech. Game text (examining things, item pick-ups, files) is read automatically and queued.

## Settings in RE2Access.ini

- `auto_walk_run=1` runs during auto-walk (set 0 to walk).
- `unlimited_health`, `unlimited_ammo`: set by F5 and F6.
- `audio_description=1` plays the audio description during the game's movies (set 0 to turn it off).
- `audio_description_offset_ms=0` moves every description line later (positive) or earlier (negative), in milliseconds, if the lines come out of step with the movie.
- `speech_volume=100` (0-200), `ducking=1`, `ducking_volume=65` (the game's volume while the mod talks, percent), `intro_description_volume=50` (the introduction descriptions, percent of the speech volume): set by Shift+F3/F4 and F2.

## Assist options

Both are off at first and are remembered between sessions (in `RE2Access.ini` in the game folder).

- **Unlimited health (F5).** Your health is topped back up the moment you take damage. You still hear the hurt sound, so you know you are being attacked.
- **Unlimited ammo (F6).** The equipped weapon keeps its loaded rounds: it never needs reloading and your spare ammunition is never used. The weapon stays at the fullest it has been since you turned the option on, so if it was part-empty, reload once and it then stays full.

## What is spoken automatically

- Title menus: main menu (Load game, Original mode, Arrange mode, Special, Option), character choice (Leon or Claire, left or right to change), difficulty (Normal or Easy, left or right to change; Hard when unlocked), and the Special menu.
- Room name and a summary of exits, items and enemies on entering a room, once the door animation and the room's own setup have finished.
- Things that appear or change while you are in a room: "New: Federal police report" when the printer has printed it, "New: Red jewel" when a puzzle releases it. Spots that hand over an item are named after the item.
- Game messages, item pick-up questions and the Yes/No cursor. Messages are read a page at a time, as the game shows them: press action to go to the next page, as you would anyway. A question is read on its page, then the highlighted choice once the Yes and No are on screen, and each choice as you move between them (the game plays its own cursor sound).
- Status screen: the top menu (File, Map, Item, Exit), the item under the cursor ("Slot N", or "Top box" for the box above the list), the command menu (Equip or Use, Check, Combine) and the second item when combining.
- Save and load screens: the list title, the highlighted entry (Create New, each saved game, Exit) and the confirmation boxes (Overwrite or Cancel, Yes or No) with the highlighted choice.
- Computer terminals: each line as the computer finishes typing it, and each letter you type at the name prompt. While a terminal is up, the mod's letter keys are switched off so you can type.
- Documents you pick up: the title, page count and each page as you turn it.
- Item box: your slot under the cursor, then the box place you would swap with ("Empty. Box 3") as you scroll.
- Stairs are in the catalog ("Stairs up", "Stairs down") and auto-walk can take you over them; the room summary mentions them.
- A zombie lying on the floor that has not got up yet is announced as "Zombie lying on the floor"; auto-walk keeps a little away from it but does not stop for it. Once it moves it counts as any other enemy.
- Enemies that are asleep until a script wakes them are left out of the catalog, the enemy count and auto-walk; you hear "Zombie is getting up" when one wakes.
- Health changes, enemy hits and kills, ammunition, wall bumps, stairs and climbable ledges.
- Aiming: "Aiming at Zombie, 3 metres" once when you raise the weapon (not again for the same target within a few seconds).
- Enemies coming within 5 metres, at most once every 15 seconds per enemy. Several at once are one line ("6 Crows close, nearest ..."), enemies on another level say "above you" or "below you", and nothing is announced while a cutscene plays.
- Danger areas (fire, steam): the room summary mentions them, you are warned when close, auto-walk goes around them, and a route that has to cross one only goes when you ask a second time. "The danger area is gone" when it is put out.
- "Picked up Acid rounds." after you take something; "Eagle stone used." when an item leaves your inventory on something in the room.
- "You are now playing as Sherry." when the story hands you another character, and "Sherry is with you now." / "Sherry is no longer with you." when a partner joins or leaves.
- Locked doors name their key when the game marks one ("Door to Library, locked, needs the Heart key").
- Stairs: auto-walk lines you up at the end of a staircase, faces along it and presses action to take it, as the game needs ("Stairs.", then "Top of the stairs" or "Bottom of the stairs"). Stairs between rooms are doors and are used like doors.
- Climbing and drops: auto-walk can climb boxes and drop down ledges on its way ("via climb" in the log), and each ledge is in the catalog under Objects.
- Doors that are a flight of stairs into another room are named "Stairs up to ..." or "Stairs down to ..." (0.7.9).
- The game's options menu (Ctrl in the game, Option on the title screen): the top menu (Key config, Screen brightness, Sound, Exit), each key config row with its key ("Aim: X"; Enter then the new key to change it), the question to keep new keys, and the sound rows (speakers, quality, music and effects volume). The brightness page is a test picture only.
- The map key (A): the game's map screen is a picture, so the mod's map (the same as Shift M) opens with it (0.8.0). Its keys work there: minus and equals for rooms, brackets for areas, apostrophe for details, Backspace for the way there, slash for help. Backslash closes the game's map and travels to the selected room. Escape closes both maps; Shift M closes only the mod's map.
- Two doors to the same room on different levels are named "..., upper level" and "..., lower level" (0.8.1, the sewers' Branch Passage and South Control Room).
- Ladders inside a room are named by their way ("Ladder up", "Ladder down"); a door that is a ladder is "Ladder up to ..." or "Ladder down to ...". Walking to something on another level of the room takes such a ladder first ("On another level: taking the ladder up first."), answering its question (0.8.1).
- Auto-walk crosses as many flights of stairs as a room needs (0.8.2; the laboratory's platform room is up two flights, along a walkway and down two).
- When something else answers the action button at a ladder (401: the warehouse door until it is opened with the lockpick), the walk answers it, walks up to the ladder again and goes on.
- An enemy on the ceiling or another level (spiders, crows) no longer stops auto-walk; it is still announced.
- Sliding along a wall or a ledge's edge without getting closer now counts as stuck, so climbs and drops are tried.
- Items found by searching a spot are named ("First aid spray, search the spot") instead of "Interactive spot".
- A crawl pipe or vent only Sherry fits through says "too small for you to enter"; a vent blocked by a fan says "can't enter yet".
- The map's route can use a lift inside a room (the Chief's Office elevator down to the hidden passage) once you have been beyond it; travel takes the elevator.
- Travel goes on past a door's own message before its question ("A ladder leads down." then "Will you go down?").
- Entering a room with enemies says "No weapon equipped." when you carry a weapon but have none equipped (not for Sherry, who has none).
- With no weapon (Sherry), auto-walk runs whenever an enemy is within 7 metres and keeps a wider berth; while the chosen way still keeps clear of a moving enemy it is kept instead of being re-planned every half second (0.8.3: the South Control Room zombie as Sherry).
- Aiming at an enemy above or below says so and how to aim there ("Hold up as well to aim up.").
- Doors you have unlocked no longer say they are locked (0.7.8 fix: the game's key flag number is the lock id without its top bit).

## Puzzle help

- **Statue puzzle (2nd-floor west stairway corridor, the red jewel).** Examine the statue holding the red jewel ("Statue holding the red jewel" in the catalog). After the inscription the game asks "Will you push the statues into place?" with its usual Yes and No. On Yes the mod puts both statues on their floor marks, the game releases the jewel, and the mod walks you to it and presses action, so the game asks "Will you take the Red jewel?". On No nothing changes, and you can push the statues yourself.

- **Reserve power panel (basement power room).** The mod explains the meter after the panel's message and reads it after each switch ("Meter 36."). The goal is 80.
- **Archive room crank.** The crank is on top of a shelf ("Crank, on top of the shelf"). Walking to it ends at the shelf below it; examine that ("A pile of investigation reports") and the mod asks "Push the stepladder here?". On Yes it moves the stepladder under the crank, walks you to it, climbs it, faces the crank and presses action so the game asks about the crank, then climbs back down.
- **Library (the Serpent stone for Claire, the Bishop plug for Leon).** The way behind the shelves is the weak floor on the upper walkway: it is in the catalog as "Weak floor, walk onto it to drop down", and walking there drops you through. The shelves are walls for auto-walk and it follows them as they move. Examining the bronze plate tells you the shelves must stand at places 2, 5 and 7 of the nine places along the wall. The power switch must be on first (a shelf switch says so when it is off); it also slides the last shelf and opens the way back. Each shelf switch says where its shelf stands now; the game's choices are Left (one place toward place 1), Right (one place toward place 9) and No.
- **Sherry's water tank (the Club key).** Ledges are in the catalog ("Ledge to drop down", "Ledge to climb up"), so you can go down into the drained tank yourself. Or, while the tank is drained, the lever's question says "Yes also lines up the three boxes below": on Yes the mod puts the three boxes along the far wall where the game wants them, the tank fills, the boxes float up into a bridge, and auto-walk takes you across to the Club key.
- **Faucets in the media room (the Cog wheel).** The faucets are named with their number and card (Faucet 12, the Queen; Faucet 13, the King; Faucet 11, the Jack). The picture's riddle gets a note: light the fireplace first (face it and use the Lighter from your items; the note says if the Lighter is in your item box), then turn on faucets 12, 13 and 11 in that order. Each faucet's question says how many have been turned in the right order so far.

- **Item-use spots.** When you carry the item a spot takes, its catalog entry says so ("Switch, use the Cog wheel here"; the sewer panel lists all four plugs), and standing there says "You can use the Cog wheel here." Walking to such a spot steps right up to it so the game takes the item.
- **Lifts.** A switch that moves you up or down a level inside a room is named "Lift" (the lab's Pump Room, the factory's turn table). Map travel takes a lift when the next door is on another level, answering its question; "Up a level" or "Down a level" when it stops.

Catalog names for things you can examine are kept short so the game's own description is still worth hearing: "Examine paper", "Examine control monitor", or "Something to examine" when the description does not name a thing. Scripted spots are named from the question they ask ("Computer").

## Known gaps (please report what you hear)

These parts were built against the simulator and the game's memory layout but have not been checked in the real game yet:

- The Special menu and the Option screen (labels for Special come from which word is lit; the Option screen is not read yet).
- Save/load screens and computer terminals were decoded from the game's code and tested in the simulator; the exact wording of saved-game entries comes from the game and will be checked in the next session's log (lines starting `[SCREEN]` and `[TERMINAL]`).
- The map and reading files from the status screen.
- The statue puzzle help was built from the game's code and tested in the simulator; lines starting `[PUZZLE]` in the log show each step.
- Auto-walk now follows the game's own collision rules, read from the game's code; lines starting `[WALK]` show each route it plans.
- The archive room stepladder help and the library's weak floor and moving shelves (0.7.8) were built from the game's code and tested in the simulator; lines starting `[PUZZLE] stepladder` and `[OBJ]` in the log show what happened.
- The map (0.7.8) is built from the room files: doors the story opens later through events may be missing until you are in that room, and a few items listed may belong to the other story path. Lines starting `[MAP]` in the log.
- The water tank help (0.7.8) was built from the game's code and tested in the simulator; `[PUZZLE] water tank` in the log.
- Exact timing of message reading. If a message is not read, press F11 while it is on screen and send the log.

## Folders

- `src`: the mod source (`win_main.cpp` is the Windows host; `mod.cpp`, `state.cpp`, `room.cpp` are the shared logic).
- `src\deploy.ps1`, `src\deploy.bat`: the deploy steps run by `Deploy.vbs`.
- `data`: room names, file texts and menu labels copied to the game folder's `RE2Access` folder.
- `build\prebuilt\RE2Access.asi`: a ready-built copy used when Visual Studio is not installed.
- `tests\harness.cpp` (and `harness.exe`): runs the real mod code inside the simulator (`python -m re2sim --mod cpp`, or `python tests\run_tests.py --cpp` in the Simulator folder).
- `tools\gen_tables.py`: regenerates `src\tables.inc` from the simulator's tables.

The lines are recordings at a fixed speed, so they always fit their gap; they are in `RE2Access\movies` in the game folder. `[MOVIE]` and `[AD]` log lines record each movie's start, every line played and how long the movie ran. Settings: `audio_description`, `audio_description_offset_ms`.
- 0.9.17: Extreme Battle's typed texts are read: the briefing at the start of a run (two pages, from "A hazardous virus has been detected..." to "GOOD LUCK!"), the stage clear screen ("Stage clear. Clear time: ...", then the next objective, "Fight to the police precinct!" or "Obtain the 4 special bombs!", and "Press any button") and the "Mission complete" text at the end (two pages, ending "CONGRATULATIONS HERO!!"). The text comes from the game's own tables. Room announcements wait until a page has been read instead of cutting it off.
- 0.9.16: Extreme Battle. Its title ("Extreme Battle title. Press any key."), its menu (Load game, Game start, Result, Exit), the level menu (Level 1 to 3 with left and right, Exit), the character select before a run (Ada, Leon, Claire, Chris, saying how many are open), "Stage clear. Time: ..." after each stage, the Result records (each level's best character and time) and the result after a run (level, character, your time and each stage's, the best time, New record, Try the next level, You are great). [EXTREME] log lines record the results.
- 0.9.15: The results screens at the end of a scenario are read, a line at a time as each appears: Congratulations, which game was cleared, the ranking (S, A, B, C or D), total time, number of saves, and on the next page any weapon you earned with "Check the item box!" and the hints for the bonus modes. The 4th Survivor and To-fu endings get their closing text and their own results the same way. [RESULT] log lines record each entry.
- 0.9.14: The 4th Survivor and To-fu title screen is read. The game shows it when you choose the mode from Special and again after the mode's ending: "4th Survivor title. Press any key.", then "Game start, plays 4th Survivor again" or "Exit to the main menu" as you press up and down. The clock those modes run is no longer read out every second as a countdown; Y says "Time so far".
- 0.9.13: Title screens. Moving on the character screen is now read (Leon, Claire, Exit), and the difficulty screen that follows it (Normal, Easy, Hard, Exit) is read when it appears and as you move. These screens take input in a later step than the main menu, which the mod was not watching. "Starting the game." is said when a new game is chosen. The game shows no description text for the title options, only their names.
- 0.9.12: Escape countdowns are spoken. When one starts you hear "Countdown: 5 minutes left. Y: time left."; then the time left at each whole minute and at 30 and 10 seconds. Y says the time left at any moment (T stays turn to the nearest enemy). The countdown stops while scenes play, as the game's does.
- 0.9.11: Leon B's escape train: once the train is moving, the last fight starts when you walk forward (west) through the connecting door into the front car. That spot had no catalog entry; it is now listed as "Scene trigger, walk into it" until the scene has played. Walking there goes straight down the middle of the car, clear of the doorway's edges.
- 0.9.10: Leon B's ending: the train's lever in the Train Control Room is listed as "Activation lever for the train motor", and until the tunnel gate is open its entry ends "the tunnel gate isn't open yet" (it was listed as "Tunnel gate"). The gate is opened from the Entrance (the room before the Train Control Room), whose switch is now listed as "Device to open the tunnel gate" (it was "Device"). Other things named by what they are for get the full name too, for example "Device to regulate the quality of sewage water".
- 0.9.9: The Pump Room in the lab (the room with the door to Power Room U): the way to that door is up from the lower floor, over a crate that a sighted player pushes off the end of the upper west walkway. The crate is now in the catalog as "Crate on the upper walkway, the mod pushes it off the end"; picking it drops the crate from wherever you are. Then pick "Door to Power Room U" from the lower floor (take the lift down first if you are on the walkway): auto-walk climbs onto the crate, up the step and onto the walkway to the door. Climbable objects like the crate now count as ledges for auto-walk, and climb and drop spots are now found the way the game checks them for the player, so some steps that were missed before are used.
- 0.9.8: Places that start a scene when you walk into them (no button) are now in the catalog as "Scene trigger, walk into it", for example the far side of the factory's second turn table, where the next scene starts after you step out of the train car. Walking there starts the scene. They leave the catalog once the scene has played. "G down." is no longer said on entering a room while its enemies are still being set up.
